About this package

Nature, coffee, charming towns and hot springs in a well-balanced itinerary. The program explores Eje Cafetero over 5D/4N, with bed-and-breakfast accommodation, transfers, guided tours and local assistance. The order of activities may change due to weather, hotel and ticket availability, or local operations.

Highlights

  • 5D/4N program with accommodation, transfers and tours included
  • Choose 3★, 4★ or 5★ hotel categories in your quotation
  • Explore Eje Cafetero with local Spanish- or English-speaking guides
  • WhatsApp assistance before and throughout the trip

Meals included

The program includes 4 breakfasts (one for each night of accommodation). Lunches and dinners shown in the itinerary are detailed in the quotation; all other meals are free so you can choose where to eat.

Transport and transfers

Includes arrival and departure transfers and ground transport for the tours in the itinerary. Domestic flights between cities, when required, are quoted separately and state baggage and ticketing.

Languages, guides and assistance

Guides and assistance in Spanish or English. Other languages (Portuguese, French, Italian) on request and subject to prior quotation. WhatsApp assistance throughout the trip.

Important before booking

  • Valid passport or ID; some tickets and flights are issued in the traveler's name.

  • Hotels, tickets and schedules are subject to availability at the time of confirmation.
  • The final quotation identifies every included, optional and non-refundable component before payment.

Arrival, departure and logistics

Transfers are coordinated according to the confirmed flight or bus schedule. For night arrivals or starts without a transfer, the meeting point and operations contact are provided. Exact details are delivered in the final voucher.

Booking and cancellation
